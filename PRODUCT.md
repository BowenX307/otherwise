# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
College students who use AI to look things up and do homework. They open OtherWise for a few minutes at a time, curious rather than rushed, usually at night on a laptop. For this hackathon the first "user" is a judge watching a 90-second demo on a projector.

## Product Purpose
OtherWise is a check-in exploration app. Your universe starts with a few planets generated from what you already read. Each check-in on a topic brings back content one step outside your usual view and unlocks a new planet, placed farther out the farther it is from your habits. Success: a judge says "wow" on first sight, then understands in seconds that the dark outer orbits are their blind spots.

## Brand Personality
Calm, alive, quietly ambitious. The universe breathes, drifts, and pulls on itself. The interface around it stays almost silent: dark ground, light text, thin lines. Color belongs only to planets, so the universe gets more colorful the more you explore.

## Anti-references
- Generic solar-system diagrams: evenly drawn rings, flat dots, static layouts.
- Inception's weaknesses: crossing connection lines, overlapping labels, a tiny cluster in an empty screen.
- AI-app tropes: rounded cards, drop shadows, gradient buttons, glass panels, emoji, icon libraries, "AI-powered" copy.

## Design Principles
1. The universe tells the story; text only confirms it.
2. Every motion means something: breathing is life, drift is time, attraction is relation, darkness is the unexplored.
3. One thing per screen, with a clear next step.
4. Color is earned by exploring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for all text. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (no drift, parallax, or breathing). Planets reachable and openable by keyboard.
